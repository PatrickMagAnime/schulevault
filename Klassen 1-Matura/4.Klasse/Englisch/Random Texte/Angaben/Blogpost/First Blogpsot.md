**Is HTL the best option for getting rich?**
Hey guys! Welcome to my Blog post about Higher Technological High schools. Have you ever heard once of HTL's? A Higher Technological High school is mostly specialised in Technological subjects such as Networking Science or Media Design. Those specific subject will definitely grant you lot of practical experience which will increase your chance in being success later in life.

If I hadn't have done one big project which was a Video Split program, I wouldn't have an outstanding understanding of the programming language Java. I may have tried to program it in Python, but i failed. So I stayed at Java.

Additionally, the next important thing to mention is that you need to work in Teams if you want to improve faster. It also increases your adaptability to other programmers which will also working together in a team with you in future.
